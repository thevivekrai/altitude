Our Story

Where the Sky Meets the Table

Perched atop Chowkidingee in the heart of Dibrugarh, ALTITUDE is more than a restaurant — it's an experience. We believe the finest meals are those shared under the open sky, with food that tells a story.

Our chefs craft every dish with seasonal ingredients and a passion for bold, authentic flavours that resonate with the spirit of Northeast India and beyond.
